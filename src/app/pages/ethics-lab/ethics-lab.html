Ethics Lab

Think before you decide.

Guided encounters for the three questions. Each one names an issue, places it in context, weighs benefits against risks, and asks you to choose between two imperfect options. There is no score. There is a record of what your choice protects and what it endangers.

01How to use the lab
01

Read the situation

Start with a concrete pressure: speed, trust, credit, simulation, force or global rules.

02

Weigh the stakes

Notice who benefits, who carries risk, and which questions remain open.

03

Choose, then inspect

Pick an option. The lab will not crown a winner. It will show the trade-off you accepted.
