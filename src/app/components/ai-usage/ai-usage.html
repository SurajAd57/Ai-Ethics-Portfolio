How Generative AI Was Used in This Portfolio

AI assisted, human reviewed.

AI was used as an assistive tool. AI-assisted output was reviewed, edited and organized by the student team. AI-generated information was not treated as automatically accurate.

IdeationBrainstorming concepts and visual directions.
Research supportHelping identify questions and areas that require verification.
Content draftingExploring first drafts and alternative explanations.
Design ideasDeveloping visual structures for diagrams and artifacts.
Angular development supportAssisting with component and template implementation.
Human reviewChecking accuracy, ethics, context and accessibility.
Final editingSelecting, rewriting and organizing the published result.

Human judgment remained responsible for the final ethical interpretation, selection of content and presentation.

AI limitations

AI can be incorrect, reproduce bias and generate misleading content. It does not replace human ethical judgment; outputs require verification and context.
